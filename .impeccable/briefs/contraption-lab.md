# 器用实验室 / Contraption Lab

Mode: Operate with an experiential simulation. Specified local extension: user selected option1, a simple Bad Piggies-like mobile assembly game for functionality. Inherit seasonal Labs world; no new world, seed or comp applies.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The same few parts acquire different functions through position, direction and task; build, launch, laugh at a tumble, then change one thing and retry.
OWN-WORLD: Incumbent exhibition palette and typography. A nine-position assembly jig, four authored mechanical SVG symbols and one explicit runfield; the vector geometry is the actual simulated machine and terrain, not decorative illustration.
STORY: Select a wheel, fan, balloon or spring; tap a mount, rotate a fan, launch. Forces, leverage, ground contact and load determine motion. Three short courses reward rolling, gliding and pushing through wind. A sample gets first action moving; failure retains the design.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Scenario above a dominant assembly jig and compact parts palette; explicit launch. Running folds assembly into an editable disclosure and gives the camera-following track the stage, with Pause/Step/Retry immediately below. Mobile mounts and actions stay44px.
FORM: User-pinned small assembly-and-run game in the existing Labs hub, not a new visual world; seed/comp skipped for this specified extension. No raster assets required for runtime mechanics.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

States: build/play/paused/won/crashed/timeout. Four attachments plus fixed cargo core. Original bounded deterministic mechanics, finite fuel and course duration, persistent validated best records independent of other labs. Context pauses never auto-resume; manual/OS motion reduction retains Step. These are fictional game mechanics, not engineering/medical predictions.
