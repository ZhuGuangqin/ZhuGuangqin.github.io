怎么玩？How to play

载荷固定在中央，再装最多4个零件。先选零件，再点安装位；风扇默认朝右。选「转向」后点风扇，每次转90度；选「拆卸」后点零件即可移除。1–4选零件，R转向，X拆卸，Enter/Space操作安装位。空白装配台可使用试玩拼装。The cargo core stays in the centre; add up to four parts. Choose a part, then tap a mount. Fans point right by default. Choose Rotate and tap a fan to turn it 90 degrees; choose Remove and tap a part to take it off. Keys 1–4 choose parts, R rotates, X removes, Enter/Space activates mounts. Try a sample on an empty jig.

轮子在下方接地时减少阻力；风扇沿箭头推送，燃料可用9秒；气囊一直上托，偏置会产生倾斜；弹簧接触地面后弹起一次。每个零件都会增加重量。落地过猛、侧翻、跌入裂谷或未及时抵达都可能失败。暂停可逐次推演0.25秒；重装保留设计，再改一个位置试试。试玩记录、数值和力学均为原创简化设定。Wheels reduce drag when mounted beneath the machine. Fans push along their arrows for nine seconds. Balloons lift continuously; offset lift can tip the craft. A spring jumps once on ground contact. Every part adds weight. Hard landings, tipping, gaps or running out of time can end a run. Step advances 0.25 seconds while paused. Rebuild keeps your design so you can change one thing. Values and physics are original simplified game rules.

为何是功能性？Why functionality?

同一个风扇在侧面朝右是推进器，换向后可以上托，偏离中心又可能把载具掀翻。零件的功能取决于它的位置、方向、其他零件及眼前的任务。这里只呈现原理的一个可玩侧面。参考Bad Piggies的拼装试跑思路，载具、地形、关卡和界面独立制作。The same fan can propel, lift or tip a machine depending on its direction and leverage. Function comes from position, orientation, other parts and the task at hand. This is one playful view of the principle. Inspired by Bad Piggies, with original machines, terrain, tracks and interface.
