怎么玩？How to play

先选守卫，再点格子种下。左侧五列可布阵，右端是扰动入口；阻止它们穿过左侧防线。每波结束会暂停，留时间调整。空闲的莲卫会蓄能，藤障会再生；枢纽感知邻路受损或密集来潮后，自行照料、减速。Choose a guard, then a cell. Plant in the first five columns; disturbances enter on the right. Stop them crossing the left edge. Waves pause between rounds. Idle Lotus stores charge; Vines regrow. Nexus senses nearby damage or dense threats, then repairs or slows them.

游团：普通；疾风：快速；重砾：高耐久。清除扰动、芽源和基础供给都会补充能量。升级花费45，回收返还一半投入。支持方向键移动、Enter/Space种植；暂停后仍可布阵、逐拍推演。Drift: ordinary. Gust: fast. Stone: durable. Energy comes from Sprouts, cleared threats and basic supply. Upgrades cost 45; recycling returns half the investment. Arrow keys navigate; Enter/Space plants. You can build and step while paused.

为何是自主性？Why autonomy?

你决定初始结构和干预时机，守卫依据局部状态自行改变行动。防线表现来自供给、阻挡、攻击与照料的协同。这是对自主调节的游戏化类比，植物、扰动和数值为虚构设定，不模拟人体或治疗。它参考分路布阵的塔防玩法，图形和机制为本站原创。You set the structure and intervene; guards change actions in response to local conditions. Supply, barriers, attacks and care work together. This fictional model explores one aspect of self-regulation, without simulating the body or treatment. It draws on lane-defence gameplay, with original artwork and mechanics.
