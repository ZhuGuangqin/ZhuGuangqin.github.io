怎么玩？How to play

先选植物卡，再点击带种植标记的空格。也可用“试玩布阵”自动布下三株莲卫和一株芽源，真实扣除220能量，再开始来潮。左侧五列可布阵，右端是扰动入口；阻止它们穿过左侧防线。每段来潮有固定守持时间，时间到且防线未崩即可完成，无须清空扰动；时段之间暂停，留时间调整。空闲的莲卫会蓄能，藤障会再生；枢纽感知邻路受损或密集来潮后，自行照料、减速。Choose a plant card, then a marked empty cell. Quick setup plants three Lotus guards and a Sprout for 220 energy; launch the wave when ready. Plant in the first five columns; disturbances enter on the right. Stop them crossing the left edge. Hold the defence for each timed period; clearing every threat is unnecessary. Periods pause between rounds. Idle Lotus stores charge; Vines regrow. Nexus senses nearby damage or dense threats, then repairs or slows them.

游团：普通；疾风：快速；重砾：高耐久。芽源每7拍供能9点，升级后14点。攻击和藤障再生各耗1点，枢纽照料或减速耗2点；每株非供能植物每12拍还需1点维持，缺能时损失4点耐久。能量不足时相应回应停止，化解扰动不奖励能量。升级花费45，回收返还一半投入。支持方向键移动、Enter/Space种植、1–4选卡、Esc回到种植；暂停后仍可布阵、逐拍推演。Drift: ordinary. Gust: fast. Stone: durable. Sprouts supply 9 energy every seven ticks, or 14 when upgraded. Shots and Vine regrowth cost 1; Nexus care or slowing costs 2. Each non-supply plant also needs 1 every twelve ticks, losing 4 durability if unfunded. Unfunded responses stop. Clearing threats gives no energy. Upgrades cost 45; recycling returns half the investment. Arrow keys navigate; Enter/Space plants; 1–4 selects a card; Esc returns to planting. You can build and step while paused.

为何是有序性？Why orderedness?

目标是让阵型在持续扰动中维持，而非消灭所有来潮。供能位置、承载前后与照料范围共同影响消耗和恢复；资源断流会使回应停止，继而破坏结构。用有限时段观察动态秩序：它需要不断维持。这里是有序性的局部游戏类比，不将游戏读数等同于“阴平阳秘”或真实人体参数。Sustain the arrangement under disturbance rather than eliminate everything. Supply placement, front-to-back structure and support range shape consumption and recovery. Lost supply stops responses and can unravel the arrangement. These finite periods explore dynamic order, with fictional values rather than physiological predictions.
