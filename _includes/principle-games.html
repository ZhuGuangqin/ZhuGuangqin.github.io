有序实验室Order Lab

有序性：构件不变，改变它们的组织方式。Orderedness: the pieces stay. Their relationships change.

双环是泉眼；实线已连通，虚线待连接。点击顺时针转动，方向键移动焦点。Double ring: spring. Solid paths connect; dashed ones wait. Click to turn clockwise; arrow keys move focus.

为何是有序性？Why orderedness?

构件并没有增加，改变的是方向和连接。局部转对，不等于整体接通。这个游戏只借用“组织关系”的一个侧面，不把连通等同于熵，也不模拟经络或人体。The pieces stay the same; their orientation and connections change. A correct local turn need not complete the whole. This explores one aspect of organisation, without equating connectivity with entropy or modelling the body.
