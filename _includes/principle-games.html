为何是有序性？Why orderedness?

构件并没有增加，改变的是方向和连接。局部转对，不等于整体接通。这个游戏只借用“组织关系”的一个侧面，不把连通等同于熵，也不模拟经络或人体。The pieces stay the same; their orientation and connections change. A correct local turn need not complete the whole. This explores one aspect of organisation, without equating connectivity with entropy or modelling the body.

为何是自主性？Why autonomy?

同样的天气，固定输入与根据自身状态调节，会走出不同的轨迹。反馈并非总比固定输入好：容量、延迟和环境都会改变结果。这里借用自主调节的思想，数值是虚构的蓄水游戏，不代表人的生理机制或调养建议。每轮规则锁定，重试可更改；对照不包含应急操作。Under the same weather, fixed input and state-based regulation can take different paths. Feedback is not always better: capacity, delay and weather matter. This is a fictional reservoir game inspired by self-regulation, not a physiological model or health advice. Rules lock for a run; retry to change them. The comparison excludes rescue actions.
