有机性：构件完好，通与不通取决于联系。Organicity: intact parts, changing connections.

双环是泉眼；实线已连通，虚线待连接。点击顺时针转动，方向键移动焦点。Double ring: spring. Solid paths connect; dashed ones wait. Click to turn clockwise; arrow keys move focus.

为何是有机性？Why organicity?

每个构件都是完整的，失效来自接口的关系。旋转只改变朝向，不增删或替换构件；一处调整会改变整张网的通路。局部接通不等于整体协调，需要把每个节点放回联系中判断。这是有机性的局部游戏类比，不模拟经络、病因或治疗。Every piece is intact. Failure lies in its connections. Rotation changes relationships without adding or replacing parts, and one turn can affect the whole network. A local connection need not complete the whole. This is a playful analogy for organicity, not a model of the body or treatment.
