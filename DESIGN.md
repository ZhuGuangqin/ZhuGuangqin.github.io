# Design System: 祝广钦 / Guangqin Zhu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "学术展厅 / 连续空间叙事"**

This site has two related surface families. The main academic pages are a quiet seasonal exhibition: paper-toned fields, serif display headings, readable records, and publications that can be taken from a shelf. Know Me at /cv/ is a deliberately independent silver/paper, yellow and black spatial narrative, using original line geometry, large bilingual type, sharp frames and continuous native scrolling. The phrase above records the accepted direction; it is not a newly proposed concept.

The durable relationship is scholarly specificity with an accessible reading route. Exhibition pages keep their established seasonal atmosphere; Know Me keeps its user-pinned line-space opening, academic blocks, cinematic journey, approaching works, birth-year and contact ending. These are separate surface grammars, not interchangeable templates. PRODUCT.md supplies content and access constraints, while the shipped CSS and scripts supply the values below.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two surface families with explicit token namespaces.
- Whole-page seasonal palettes on the exhibition; a stable light/dark neutral frame with seasonal accent on Know Me.
- Original mathematical line geometry and native scroll-driven perspective.
- Public-source records, truthful cover provenance, and direct reading alternatives.
- Local fonts/assets, bilingual controls, keyboard/touch routes and reduced-motion reading.

## Colors

The exhibition uses eight complete seasonal light/dark assignments; Know Me uses a stable paper/ink or dark/silver frame and one season-sensitive accent. Frontmatter is normative. All color values come from the shipped CSS, with no inferred replacement palette or synthetic tonal ramp.

### Primary

- **Know Me seasonal light:** `cv-acid-spring`, `cv-acid-summer`, `cv-acid-autumn`, `cv-acid-winter` map to the source `--cv-acid`. They fill the chapter strip, system block, cinema and contact field. Dark ink stays legible over them.
- **Exhibition seasonal accent:** `exhibition-{season}-{mode}-accent` maps to `--accent`; it controls links, selected options, stars and actions within that exact palette.

### Neutral

- **Know Me paper / dark ink:** `cv-paper` and `cv-dark` exchange `--cv-bg` and `--cv-fg` between day and night. The same dark ink remains on accent surfaces in both modes.
- **Know Me secondary materials:** mode-suffixed `cv-muted`, `cv-rule`, `cv-line` and `cv-panel` describe metadata, hairline frames, artwork strokes and dossier/citation fills. `cv-accent-ink-light` is the fixed daytime accent ink; at night `--cv-accent-ink` uses the current seasonal accent.
- **Exhibition field family:** each `exhibition-{season}-{mode}-{canvas,panel,field,ink,muted,line,button-ink}` is the corresponding source custom property, not an independently chosen color. Canvas extends across the page; panel supports surfaces; field separates controls/footer; line divides records; ink and muted distinguish reading levels.

Exhibition state lookup uses the frontmatter prefix below; all eight role values for each prefix are recorded there.

| --- | --- | --- |

The CSS owns runtime assignments. On Know Me, the body-scoped `--canvas`, `--panel`, `--ink`, `--muted`, `--field`, `--accent`, `--line` and global theme aliases resolve to its CV family. Season/theme preferences remain shared across routes. Frontmatter components show the default spring/light assignment; the sidecar snippets retain the live variables and dark/season overrides.

**The Surface Boundary Rule.** Keep the exhibition and Know Me token families separate. A square spatial CV frame is not a replacement for the established exhibition shelf or folio.

**The Day Ink / Night Silver Rule.** Know Me switches the entire neutral frame between paper/ink and dark/silver. Seasonal accent changes do not replace the daytime ink token.

## Typography

**Know Me display and body:** local Space Grotesk (`CV Grotesk`) paired with local Noto Sans SC (`CV Sans SC`), with sans-serif fallback. Variable faces expose weights 300–700 and 400–800 respectively. The interface uses actual weight and size assignments rather than a new scale ratio.

**Exhibition display:** local `Research Display`, then `Songti SC`, then serif. **Exhibition body:** the incumbent platform sans stack, recorded in `exhibition-body`. The system body fallback remains appropriate for reading; it is not the display direction for new headings.

### Hierarchy

- **Know Me identity:** `cv-display`; English uses `cv-display-en` so the real name fits without losing hierarchy. At 700px and below, Chinese uses the source 76px identity; English uses `clamp(34px,10.5vw,65px)`. Narrow and short-height overrides remain in source.
- **Know Me section headings:** `cv-headline`; the blocks, cinema, tunnel and contact have their evidenced role-specific variants. Film titles use `cv-film-title`. Contact has `cv-contact-display`, while layered birth-year numerals use `cv-since-year`.
- **Know Me text:** `cv-body` for readable education/experience detail; compact controls and metadata generally use 11–13px source assignments. Reader prose is 13px with line-height 1.9; native dialog title uses `cv-reader-title`.
- **Exhibition headings:** `exhibition-display` for the home hero; `exhibition-title` for page titles. The hero retains its serif voice and tight tracking.
- **Exhibition text:** `exhibition-body`, with page reading measure capped at 75ch on the desktop grid. Folio text uses line-height 1.9; publication captions are compact but complete detail remains accessible.

Do not add a mono role or invented scale ratio: neither defines the shipped system. Existing keyboard hints can use their local monospace assignment without creating a display family.

**The Native Camera Rule.** Native vertical scrolling advances the spatial scenes. Preserve document scrolling and the direct controls; do not add wheel or touch scroll interception.

## Elevation & Depth

Know Me uses frames, linework and selective projection rather than broad card shadows. Academic coordinates are flat: no block rotation, translation or perspective. They reveal downward through a scroll-driven clip mask, with all panels open at the reading stop. The remaining spatial effects use cover overlap, concentric tunnel wires and layered outlined numerals; perspectives remain 1000px for the tunnel and 900px for birth-year. The work face keeps a single rule; the broad face shadow removed in the scored fix is not part of this system.

The exhibition retains the physical shelf vocabulary. The source shadow values live in `.impeccable/design.json`: low ambient dock/menu lift; angled volume resting lift; a stronger taken-volume hover; and the reader overlay. Owner-supplied book silhouettes use the source drop-shadow, no outer book shell, with a metadata-defined crop. Dialog backdrops blur the surrounding world; they are top-layer reading surfaces, not permanent decorative panels.

**The Material Depth Rule.** Keep academic coordinates flat with a downward reveal. Projection, overlap, linework and perspective provide depth in the other Know Me scenes. The exhibition shelf may use its existing physical volume shadows; do not restore the removed broad shadow to Know Me cover faces.

## Shapes

Know Me uses square corners, 1px frames and fine continuous paths. Rectangles contain content while linework, rings and projected forms move within them. The original “中”/orbit/frame mark is a personal signature; it is not a university crest. The loader draws paths, rather than pretending to report progress.

Exhibition controls are gently rounded; frontmatter records the actual control, panel, reader and dock radii. Journal volumes use an asymmetric shallow edge and a 2:3 front face. Original owner book photographs retain their real perspective silhouette: `shape_style` in `_data/publication_covers.json` supplies per-image ratio, position and polygon clipping, with unchanged source pixels. Those book polygons are asset-specific data, not reusable shape tokens. Circles, arcs and organic seasonal orbit changes are native to this world and must not be prohibited by a general “no circles” rule.

## Components

### Buttons, fields and navigation

Know Me print is a square seasonal-accent action with dark ink, 15px / 20px padding and a minimum 44px height. Hover returns it to the panel/foreground pairing. Pulse, pause/read and chooser use rule strokes and current frame colors; the chooser is a full row above actions on mobile. Disabled states fade to .45. CV focus uses a 2px current-color outline offset 6px; the main exhibition focus uses a 3px accent outline offset 4px.

The chapter strip is sticky, accent-filled and bold; current/hover links underline rather than introducing a pill. It retains native hashes, modified-click navigation and a destination focus path. The brief slice transition is skipped when motion is disabled. Main-site masthead and reading tools retain their rounded exhibition controls and persisted preferences. Exhibition search/filter fields, selected view options and action buttons use their active palette; do not substitute the CV square control treatment globally.

### Academic blocks and cinema

The blocks link actual research directions, public papers, existing CNKI records, participation in funding, a book record and education. Their nonuniform spans and edge frames form a flat composition. Native scrolling opens each panel from top to bottom through `clip-path:inset(0 0 var(--block-mask,0%) 0)`; the cubic mask reaches zero before the reading stop, with no departure fold. Focus reveals the whole selected block; motion-off and no-JS remove clipping. A measured stage taller than the available viewport, including localized or short-desktop layouts, receives `.is-flowing` and reads in normal document flow; mobile always does so. Content order and complete descriptions are preserved. Hover uses the seasonal accent with dark ink. Count/title prominence does not authorize new claims. The cinema uses real `_data/cv_journey.yml` entries, geometric year panels, headings and detail paragraphs; event kind is below the heading, with no generic eyebrow. Vertical scrolling translates the track; previous/next and focused-frame arrow keys provide explicit routes. Frame changes commit synchronously so rapid successive actions advance correctly.

### Work tunnel and native readers

Public works approach through a wire cylinder; the live chooser identifies a stable selection and translates at boot as well as on language change. Pause, choose, read, direct list, click and horizontal drag are parallel access routes. Cards preserve source labels and original cover treatment. Details are native dialogs containing original text, citation/PDF/source actions and a sticky close control. Close/Escape restores the originating cover focus; with motion enabled and the tunnel visible, its face dissolves into 30 clipped fragments. Reduced/static reading omits dissolve. The reader is real content, not a decorative hover preview.

### Exhibition shelf, folios and cultural star chart

The shelf offers take/read and a compact list view. The active item lifts; a native reader provides source text and citation actions, and closes back to its origin. Project/teaching folios are native expandable dossiers with line-led summaries and a panel body. Topics are text-filtered public records, not a claim of causal or semantic-model linkage. At night, the four cultural groups expose 28 mansions on desktop; the final 600px-and-below treatment shows the selected seven with explicit four-group controls. Chart points/lines are cultural artwork, never realtime observations.

Image source truth is part of the surface: 14 topic artworks use the exact maintained prompts in `docs/cover-generation.json`; five owner-supplied books use `docs/book-cover-sources.json`; two verified issue originals use their source records in `_data/publication_covers.json`; two remaining works use labeled record cards. All 21 web JPEGs have embedded prompt/origin data according to the accepted scan (0 missing). Generated covers are topic metaphors, not publisher covers or evidence. This document references that provenance instead of copying or rewriting it.

### Birth-year, contact and opening signature

The layered 1998 scene includes the explicit born-in-1998 caption. It does not date research activity to birth. Contact uses the accent field, oversized type, a pointer-responsive line field, direct email, copy, GitHub, back-to-opening and replay controls. The original loader signature has skip, bounded timing and CSS failure release; system reduction/manual-off bypass it. Opening drawings must never gate access to facts.

**The Readable Edition Rule.** System reduction, manual motion-off and focus mode remove long pinned reading and continuous effects. Keep the original records and explicit actions available.

Motion values, breakpoints, shadow vocabulary and eight representative self-contained preview snippets are in the sidecar. Snippets preserve real source treatments and use inline SVG where an icon is useful; they introduce no new runtime component library. Primitive colors/type/spacing/radii live only in this frontmatter. The sidecar carries state/source mappings and extension properties, with no fabricated tonal scale.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the matching surface namespace and the complete observed mode/season assignments.
- **Do** keep ink text and lines on the light Know Me canvas, silver on dark, and black text on the seasonal accent blocks.
- **Do** preserve the original document records, bilingual chooser initialization, explicit previous/next controls, pause/select/read controls and focus return.
- **Do** retain distinct source labels for verified issue covers, owner-supplied book covers, topic artwork and records without a cover.
- **Do** keep original book pixels and the metadata-defined silhouette crop; put provenance in both the maintained source records and web-image metadata.
- **Do** maintain the shared mobile tool reservation and the readable linear/print paths when extending a scene.

### Don't:
- **Don't** merge the exhibition palettes into the Know Me frame or generalize a page-specific scene into a mandatory global layout.
- **Don't** interpret mathematical geometry as medical evidence or the cultural star chart as a realtime sky.
- **Don't** style generic event-type kickers above cinema headings; the current event kind belongs in the detail paragraph. Retain the explicitly requested SINCE literal.
- **Don't** turn icon-font glyphs or inherited system display faces into a new visual-system rule; extend the local display fonts and inline SVG assets instead.
- **Don't** hide the direct reading routes behind motion, chase-only covers or the opening mark, or fabricate achievements/publication status.
